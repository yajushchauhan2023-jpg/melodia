How it works

A simple bridge between students and teachers.

For students

  1. Search for a suitable teacher.
  2. Review profiles and availability.
  3. Book and pay securely on Melodia.
  4. Attend lessons and track your progress.

For teachers

  1. Create an account.
  2. Complete your teacher profile.
  3. Set lesson prices and availability.
  4. Receive and manage bookings.
  5. Teach and receive payouts.
